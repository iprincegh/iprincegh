# About Me:
 <br>Geoinformatics and Spatial Data Science researcher with interests spanning spatial modelling, spatial statistics, GIS, computational public health, earth observation, and remote sensing. My research uses spatial data and quantitative methods to study disease dynamics, human mobility, and environmental systems; currently integrating multi-source population flow data with Bayesian spatiotemporal modelling to understand infectious disease transmission dynamics at fine spatial scales. I also work on reproducible research, open science, and the development of spatial decision-support tools, combining spatial statistics, GIS, and data engineering with programming for scalable analytics across public health and environmental applications.<br>
